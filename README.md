## 📌 About the Project

**PrepAI** is an AI-powered interview preparation portal developed to transform the traditional interview preparation process into an interactive and personalized learning experience.

The platform helps users prepare for technical and HR interviews, practice AI-generated interview questions, analyze resumes, and receive AI-generated feedback on their interview performance.

With features such as voice-based interviews and performance tracking, PrepAI aims to provide users with a structured environment to identify their strengths, improve their weaknesses, and become more confident in facing real-world interviews.

🚧 **Project Status: Currently Under Development**

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. 🤖 AI-Generated Interview Questions
- Generate interview questions using Artificial Intelligence.
- Practice questions based on different topics.
- Support structured interview preparation.

### 2. 💻 Technical Interview Preparation
- Practice programming and technical interview questions.
- Strengthen technical knowledge.
- Prepare for software development and IT-related interviews.

### 3. 🗣️ HR Interview Preparation
- Practice commonly asked HR interview questions.
- Improve communication and interview confidence.
- Prepare for behavioral and personal interview rounds.

### 4. 📄 AI Resume Analysis
- Analyze uploaded resumes.
- Identify strengths and areas for improvement.
- Provide suggestions to improve resume quality.

### 5. 📊 AI-Generated Feedback
- Receive feedback based on interview responses.
- Identify areas that require improvement.
- Get personalized suggestions for better preparation.

### 6. 🎙️ Voice-Based Interview
- Experience interactive voice-based interview practice.
- Practice answering questions verbally.
- Develop communication and speaking confidence.

### 7. 🔐 User Authentication
- User registration and login.
- Individual user accounts.
- Personalized interview preparation experience.

### 8. 📈 Performance Tracking
- Track interview practice progress.
- Monitor performance over time.
- Identify strengths and weaknesses.
